Ruth Goldberg Machine

Engineered an amusement park themed Rube Goldberg machine using six simple machines, multiple energy transfers, and an autonomous robot car with mechanisms controlled by Arduino programs.

View Details
×

Ruth Goldberg Machine

Links: View Demo and Documentation

Team: Celine Semaan, Trevor Lau, Jenna Rutberg, Alexander Williamson

Description:
  • Project demonstrated physics and engineering principles through a Rube Goldberg machine that simulates an amusement park using various interconnected components.
  • Included six fundamental mechanical devices:
    • Catapult: Utilizes a lever mechanism powered by gravity to launch a projectile, demonstrating the principles of torque and angular acceleration.
    • Loop: Features a circular track where a marble gains enough velocity from a downhill ramp to navigate the loop without falling, illustrating centripetal force and energy conservation.
    • Drawbridge: Operates as both an inclined plane and a pulley system, where a marble triggers the drawbridge to lower, showing mechanics of potential and kinetic energy conversion. Arduino controls the motor that operates the drawbridge.
    • Ferris Wheel: Designed as a wheel and axle, this component is driven by a motor controlled by Arduino, allowing it to rotate and exemplify rotational motion and the effects of centripetal acceleration on the marbles.
    • Elevator: Acts as a lifting mechanism that elevates a marble to a higher point using a pulley system, highlighting the mechanical advantage provided by pulleys. The elevator's motor is also controlled by an Arduino to ensure precise operation.
    • Seesaw: Incorporates a lever that tips when a marble is added to one side, balancing forces and showcasing how levers can be used to amplify small movements into larger actions.
    • Autonomous Robot Car: Navigates around the system under Arduino control, engaging various components like the merry-go-round to complete the machine’s sequence.
    • Merry-Go-Round: Operates as a wheel & axle, with the merry-go-round spinning due to the external force of the autonomous robot car, highlighting the principles of rotational dynamics and force transfer.
  • Designed transitions between each component to ensure smooth operation and continuous motion throughout the system.
  • Project adapted to the challenges of the COVID-19 pandemic, with two out of four team members contributing remotely, necessitating robust virtual communication and collaboration skills to maintain project momentum and integrate their contributions effectively.

AllSimpleRecipes

Developed a Chrome extension that enhances the recipe browsing experience on AllRecipes.com by integrating a customizable filter menu, ingredient scaling, and an ad-blocker to streamline and personalize user interactions.

View Details
×

AllSimpleRecipes

Link: View Extension and Demo

Team: Celine Semaan, Serena Frost

Description:
  • Developed a Chrome extension that modifies the user interface of AllRecipes.com to enhance readability and usability by removing ads and redundant content.
  • Implemented a customizable filter menu within the extension, allowing users to personalize the visibility of nutrition facts, reviews, introductory paragraphs, videos, and step-by-step images based on their preferences.
  • Incorporated a dynamic ingredient scaling feature using JavaScript, which recalculates ingredient quantities based on user-selected serving sizes, using fraction notation for precision and grammatical correctness in measurements.
  • Enhanced the extension to support direct page modification rather than pop-up modals, giving users more control over content presentation and improving the aesthetic integration of information on the webpage.
  • Planned future updates to include universal metric conversions, an allergy alert system, recipe bookmarking, and customizable recipe layouts, aiming to expand the extension's utility across various cooking websites and blogs.
Artist Statement

“All Simple Recipes” is a Chrome extension designed to simplify the recipe browsing experience on the popular food-focused website, All Recipes, through a customizable filter menu, an ingredient scaling feature, and an integrated ad-blocker for a cleaner viewing experience. The shared experience of scrolling through ad-filled pages, verbose text, and unending slides of pictures before finally reaching the recipe card was the driving force that led to the creation of “All Simple Recipes.”

Upon researching existing solutions, we found “Jump To Recipe” offered by Steven Lam, a popup-style extension that displays quick recipe notes. While effective, we brainstormed areas for improvement, particularly in information organization and personalization. We decided to take a different approach: rather than using a popup modal to display information, we decided to modify the page directly, granting greater control over content presentation and visual appeal. This process enabled us to redact any ad placements completely and simplify extraneous elements while allowing users to scroll freely on the website.

Understanding the diversity of user preferences on which information is most vital, we included a customizable filtering menu within the extension popup. The five fields currently provided in this menu include nutrition facts, reviews, introductory paragraphs, videos, and step-by-step images. We also injected a serving size scaler into the existing website using Javascript, which automatically recalculates the ingredient quantities based on a chosen multiplier. To ensure readability, we used fraction notation and accounted for grammatical changes in measurements as thoroughly as possible.

Originally tailored for All Recipes, there is potential to adapt “All Simple Recipes” for broader use across various cooking websites and blogs. Future versions of this extension will strive to include universal metric conversions, an allergy alert system, recipe bookmarking, and customizable recipe layouts.

While currently in development, the extension is being prepared for publication in the Chrome Web Store. This achievement would open up a stream of real user feedback, which will serve as invaluable insights in areas for refinement and innovation. With every version update, we envision “All Simple Recipes” becoming a vital companion for culinary enthusiasts worldwide, simplifying their cooking journeys.

Fundamentally, we hope that “All Simple Recipes” can simplify the online recipe-gathering experience, offering users greater control in accessing desired information efficiently.

Artist Statement

“HEOUT” is a satirical digital experience that exposes the ethical and environmental shortcomings of the fast fashion industry by specifically targeting the fast fashion giant SHEIN. The website mirrors SHEIN’s design, from its header to its footer, to offer visitors a familiar yet unsettling journey through seemingly trendy fashion that gives way to the brutal reality of design theft, cultural appropriation, product toxicity, and environmental damage.

Incoming users are drawn into a parody of online shopping where selecting a product leads them to a unique product page that is filled with insightful descriptions as well as links to learn more about the issue represented. The pricing of the merchandise features extreme discounts which are meant to mock SHEIN’s devaluation of labor and original designs that the company has stolen.

The website’s footer contains links to an ‘About HEOUT’ page that serves as a satirical take on SHEIN’s own ‘About Us’ page, along with shipping information that redirects the visitor to an article on the impact of fast fashion shipping.

HEOUT’s purpose is to challenge consumers to consider their role and impact in the fashion industry. By using an interactive shopping experience, the project aims to spread more awareness, invoke conscious fast fashion consumer behavior, and advocate for a shift in the fashion industry towards more sustainable options.

Beyond Sight and Sound

Team: Celine Semaan, Mabel Xu

Description:

  • Integrated real and virtual elements using ShapesXR's pass-through feature, grounding the immersive user experience in reality.
  • Designed a virtual lobby with portals to six unique worlds, each representing different sensory disabilities.
  • Incorporated teleportation for movement within the XR environment, enhancing accessibility by allowing exploration without physical constraints.
  • Implemented viewpoint features in ShapesXR to guide users through a sequenced path, with the option for free exploration.
Artist Statement

“Beyond Sight and Sound” is an immersive mixed-reality project crafted using ShapesXR that invites the user to navigate a series of worlds, each simulating different visual and auditory disabilities. The inspiration for this project stems from a desire to generate a deeper understanding of the challenges individuals with disabilities experience.

Throughout our brainstorming process, we were intrigued by the potential of mixed reality to blur the lines between a tangible and virtual world. ShapesXR allowed us to sculpt an environment where the real and the imagined co-exist by offering a pass-through feature that allowed us to ground this experience in the real world.

In this project, the user begins the journey in a VR lobby room from which six unique worlds branch out. Each of these worlds is accessible through portals which are designed to introduce the user to different single and combined disabilities. We utilized the viewpoints feature of ShapesXR, which allowed us to create a numbered sequence to guide the viewer through our world. These viewpoints act as a recommended path. The users have the freedom to turn off these guidelines if they prefer to explore the world on their own. The use of teleportation to these viewpoints enables users to move around in the virtual environment without the need for physical space in the real world, which makes this experience more accessible.

The motivation for “Beyond Sight and Sound” is twofold: shine a light on the challenges of people with disabilities and celebrate the richness of different perceptions of the human experience. This work is influenced by artists and activists who aim to encourage a dialogue around this subject rooted in comprehension rather than apprehension.

The goal of this project is for the participant to come out of this journey with an appreciation and understanding of the complex sensory experiences of people with disabilities. By presenting those various worlds side by side, we want to provoke the user into questioning the diverse ways in which we interact with our environment on a day-to-day basis.

  • Headset Technology: The video was recorded and the experience was designed using the QuestMeta VR Headsets, specifically the QuestMeta 3 and QuestMeta 2 headsets.
  • Background Music: The backdrop music is "Autumn" from Antonio Vivaldi’s Four Seasons.
  • Project Title: "Beyond Sight and Sound" was brainstormed and refined with the assistance of Open AI’s ChatGPT.
